Five tempos, one floor.

Low 118 · 55+ Low 120

One foot stays on the floor. Marches, step-touches, grapevines, V-steps, hamstring curls. Lorna's 55+ class runs two beats faster because, in her words, "they get bored."

  • 50 min, 30 min at lunch
  • No platforms
  • Chairs along the mirror if you need one

Step 126

Thirty-two platforms, risers stacked to 4, 6 or 8 inches. Kenji cues from the top of the platform, always on count seven, always with his hand.

  • Beginners: 4 inches, no exceptions for two weeks
  • Leave the platform where you found it

Hi/Lo 132 · Friday Count-In

Low-impact blocks with high-impact options called as "or" — "knee up, or jog it." Friday at 7 p.m. Marcus teaches one member's cue card to the whole room, then the room votes on keeping it in rotation.

  • 50 min · Count-In 60 min
  • Bring a cue card from the Cue Card page

Interval 138

Three minutes of high-impact combos, one minute marching. Six rounds. The loudest class we run; the neighbours downstairs get a gift card every December.

  • 45 min
  • Water, towel, and two weeks of Hi/Lo first

What to bring

Cross-trainers or aerobic shoes with a pivot point under the ball of the foot. Running shoes grip too much for turns. Leotards welcome, never required.

  • Lockers: bring a lock
  • Water fountain by the door
