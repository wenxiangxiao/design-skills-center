Cue cardFour eights · one minute of floor

  1. Put one move in each of the four eights (tap a move, or press keys 1–8; Backspace takes the last one out).
  2. Pick a class tempo and press play: the floor on the left dances your combo to the beat, count by count.
  3. When the last eight lands you get a printed cue card. Bring it to the desk before Friday's Count-In.
Tempo

Four empty eights. Start with a march — marching is always legal.
