The cue

The instructor calls the next move on count seven — "grapevine, right!" — so your feet land it on the next one. You will hear a cowbell there in our house combo. Miss it and just march; marching is always legal.

The floor

Rock maple strips over a basket-weave of sleepers on neoprene pads, laid in 2011 by a crew that normally builds theatre stages. It gives about a quarter inch. Your knees will notice before you do.
