First-class questions

I can't follow choreography.

Nobody can the first time. Every move changes on count one and the cue comes on count seven, so you always get a two-beat warning. If you are lost, march. Marching is always legal.

Hi/Lo or Low — which one first?

Low 118 if you haven't jumped on purpose in a while; Hi/Lo 132 if you have. Every high-impact move in Hi/Lo is called with a low option.

What do the step heights mean?

Four, six or eight inches of platform. Beginners stay at four for two weeks. Kenji checks.

Can I bring a cue card from the website?

Yes — show it at the desk on your phone or write the code on paper (it looks like MA.GV.KN.JJ@132). Marcus picks one each Friday.

Is the music loud?

Around 85 dB at the back wall, 90 in Interval. We keep foam earplugs by the water fountain.
